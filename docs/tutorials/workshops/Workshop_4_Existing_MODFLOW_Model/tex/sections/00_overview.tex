\begin{goal}
\textbf{What you do.} A colleague has built a MODFLOW~6 model of Tutorial Creek. It works in feet, on a grid of its own,
with its own recharge, evapotranspiration (ET), rivers, well and head boundary. You couple the Raven model of Tutorial
Creek to it without changing it. You link the HRUs to its cells, let Raven take over the recharge and the ET, and send its
river flows to Raven's river reaches. Then you add ET from the water table, a wetland and a reservoir, and check the
result.

\textbf{What you need.} A Linux computer (x86\_64) with a terminal, or Windows with WSL. Python~3 with numpy, pandas and
matplotlib (Step~\ref{st:python}). A text editor, for example \cmd{nano}. Workshop~2 is helpful but not needed.

\textbf{Time.} About 40 minutes.
\end{goal}
