\section{Switch the coupling on}

\step{Edit \texttt{Creek.rvi}}\label{st:rvi}
\diff{t2_rvi.diff}
\why \cmd{:GroundwaterModel MODFLOW6} switches the coupling on. \cmd{:DefineHRUGroups} declares the group \cmd{AquiferHRUs}
of Step~\ref{st:rvh}. In the coupled HRUs the subsoil now percolates to \cmd{GROUNDWATER}, Raven's store for
the water that MODFLOW receives as recharge every day. The ridge keeps its \cmd{DEEP\_STORE}. Groundwater that seeps out at
the surface returns to the lowest soil layer (\cmd{DEEP\_STORE}), and its baseflow process takes it to the creek. The
exchange with the river cells goes straight to the river reaches.

Or copy the finished file: \cmd{cp \$WS/model/finished/Creek.rvi .}
\begin{trouble}
Raven stops when:
\begin{itemize}
\item a process takes water \emph{out} of \cmd{GROUNDWATER} in a coupled HRU (MODFLOW computes that water there);
\item no process sends water to \cmd{GROUNDWATER};
\item a process sends water to \cmd{GROUNDWATER} in an HRU without an \cmd{AQUIFER\_PROFILE} (the message ends with
  ``\dots which has no AQUIFER\_PROFILE'').
\end{itemize}
Limit such processes to the right HRUs with \cmd{:-->Conditional}, as in this change.
\end{trouble}
