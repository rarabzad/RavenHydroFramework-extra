\section{Read the well and pond series}

\step{Look at \texttt{CreekGW.rvt}}
The series file is already in the folder. Show its first lines:
\term{t2_gwrvt_head.txt}
\why \cmd{:WellFlowRate 1 m3/d} is the rate of the well with ID~1 in \cmd{:Wells}, and \cmd{:BoundaryHead POND m} the
head of the boundary named POND. Both are daily series. The second line gives the start date and time, the interval
(1~day) and the number of values.

\step{Edit \texttt{Creek.rvt}}
Tell Raven to read the file:
\diff{t2_rvt.diff}
Or copy the finished file: \cmd{cp \$WS/model/finished/Creek.rvt .}
