\subsection{Soil zone: negative recharge, MODFLOW's residual and the gross flow}\label{sec:szbooks3}
The recharge identity also subtracts the recharge MODFLOW did not apply (an existing model without \cmd{NEWTON}, signed):
\[\textstyle V_{\mathrm{Raven}}=V_{\mathrm{MF6}}+\Delta V_{\mathrm{delay}}+V_{\mathrm{CHD}}+V_{\mathrm{rej}}+(V^{uz}_{\mathrm{in}}-V^{uz}_{\mathrm{to\ aq}})+V_{\mathrm{not\ applied}},\]
and the soil-zone identity adds the cut of a negative recharge the soil zone did not hold, $V^{nh}$:
\[\textstyle \Delta W\,A/1000=W_{\mathrm{proc}}+V_{\mathrm{rej}}-\sum q\,\Delta t-V_{\mathrm{ovf}}+V^{nh}.\]
$V_{\mathrm{rej}}$ is negative where a negative recharge was more than the top cell could give (the cut is taken back out of
the soil zone). Shortfall is 0 whenever the interface converges; it appears only on steps whose interface did not converge
(most at a low outer-iteration limit, also on MODFLOW-converged steps whose last update was an early, unlimited one). The
unsaturated columns' balance $\Delta V^{uz}=V^{uz}_{\mathrm{in}}-V^{uz}_{\mathrm{to\ aq}}-V^{uz}_{\mathrm{cap}}$ is checked from
step 1, with the storage at the start that \cmd{GWModelSummary.txt} gives (``unsaturated storage''); the audit tool checks it.

MODFLOW's residual is one sum for every case that knows the storage flow, the soil-zone terms included (a steady existing
model without \cmd{STO6} has storage 0). \cmd{GWBudget.csv}'s ``MF6 balance error [\%]'' is $100$ times that residual over the
gross flow through the aquifer: every entry's inflow and outflow apart (recharge per cell, storage per cell, each well, GHB,
specified-head, river, drain, reservoir and evaporation entry, each soil-zone link net, every entry of the existing model's
own packages and its mover), MODFLOW's TOTAL IN plus TOTAL OUT, at least 1~m$^3$/d, computed before the guard; it is half
MODFLOW's PERCENT DISCREPANCY, which divides by the mean of IN and OUT. The audit tool's cumulative ratio uses signed package
nets, a stricter figure. Its guard count counts both warnings: ``(MODFLOW unconverged): discharge returned to Raven limited''
(without a soil zone) and ``of discharge to Raven withheld'' (\cmd{:SoilZone PHYSICAL}).
